The words stay here.

Loquara runs the speech model on your computer. A dictation never leaves the machine, and there is no account to create or word count to watch.

Private by default

Audio, text, and history stay on this computer. The engine runs locally, and the app never asks you to sign in.

One download, then yours

One model download buys the whole app. It does not count minutes, words, or requests.

Yours to change

MIT source. Change the shortcut, the vocabulary, the modes, or the code itself.

Already fast

The model is local, so the only wait is the time it takes to speak.

One shortcut.
A 136 px recorder.

Hold to talk or tap to toggle. The recorder floats over any app, then disappears when the words land. Terminals, IDEs, browsers, chat, and every text field work the same way.

CtrlSpace or your own shortcut

Ctrl + Shift + Space on macOS. Push-to-talk is one setting away.
