The whole idea

Dictation should feel like thinking out loud. Not another tab, another subscription, another upload.

The interface

A pill, not a panel.

One shortcut. One small floating recorder. Your words land at the caret, in the app you already use.

  • Stays out of the way. A 136 px recorder, not a window to manage.
  • Lands where you type. Clipboard, terminals and SSH included.
  • Yours to shape. Custom vocabulary, modes and shortcuts.

Why Loquara

Quietly better.

No account to create, no audio to upload, no word count to watch.

01

It stays here.

Audio, transcripts and history never leave your machine. No telemetry, no “improve the product”, no quiet upload.

02

It keeps up.

Parakeet TDT runs on your GPU or CPU. The model is already local, so the only wait is the time it takes to speak.

03

It's yours.

MIT-licensed and built to be changed. New mode, new shortcut, new language — edit it, fork it, keep it.
